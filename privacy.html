Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Curious Nest ("we", "us") makes animated learning videos for young children and publishes them on YouTube. This policy explains what information we handle on this website, on our YouTube channel, and in Curious Nest Uploader, the small tool we use to upload our own videos. The short version: we do not collect personal information from anyone, and especially not from children.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It has no cookies, analytics, advertising, sign-ups, forms or comments, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts. We do not collect any information about visitors.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses to keep its service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our YouTube videos

Our videos are hosted and played by YouTube, a Google service, and are marked "Made for Kids". On Made for Kids videos YouTube turns off comments and personalised advertising. When you watch our videos, YouTube handles your information under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive any personal information about viewers. YouTube only shows us totals, such as how many times a video was watched.

Curious Nest Uploader and YouTube API Services

Curious Nest Uploader is an internal tool used only by the Curious Nest team. It is not offered to the public. It uses YouTube API Services to publish Curious Nest videos to the Curious Nest YouTube channel.

  • What it accesses. After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the tool uploads our videos, sets their thumbnails and captions, and reads our own channel's basic details (name, ID, video and subscriber counts) to confirm it is uploading to the right channel. After each upload it reads that video's status, to confirm it was scheduled correctly.
  • What it does not do. It does not read, collect, store or share any information about other YouTube users or about people who watch our videos.
  • What it stores. The Google sign-in token, kept only on our own computer in a file only our account can read, and a list of the IDs and links of the videos we uploaded. Nothing is stored on any server, and nothing is sold or shared.
  • How access ends. The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, we delete the stored token.

By using YouTube, including through our videos, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Our videos are made for children. We never knowingly collect personal information from children: there are no accounts, comments, contact forms or tracking on our website, and comments are off on our videos. If you are a parent or teacher and have any concern, please email us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
